# Concept Taxonomy

The concepts in the learning graph are grouped into the following categories. Each category has a short TaxonomyID that appears in `learning-graph.csv` and a color in the graph viewer.

The **Advanced Path Mathematics** category is the optional advanced chapter. No concept in any other category depends on it.

## Python Setup and Tools

**TaxonomyID:** PYS

Review of the Learning Python skills plus the tooling (virtual environments, pip, packages, JSON, command line, git) that every lab uses.

## Robot Arm Fundamentals

**TaxonomyID:** ARM

Links, joints, degrees of freedom, workspace, payload, repeatability, leader and follower arms, and the vocabulary of arm design.

## Actuators and Sensors

**TaxonomyID:** ACT

Servos, brushless actuators, gear ratios, torque, encoders, feedback, and the control that holds a joint in position.

## Communication Buses and Protocols

**TaxonomyID:** COMM

Serial and CAN buses, packets, bytes, registers, device IDs, and the Python libraries that talk to motors.

## Power Systems

**TaxonomyID:** POWR

Voltage, current, power supplies, wiring, fuses, distribution, and the electrical problems that cause failures.

## Safety

**TaxonomyID:** SAFE

Pinch points, limits, emergency stops, safe power-up and shutdown, supervision, and fail-safe design.

## Sourcing Parts

**TaxonomyID:** SORC

Bills of materials, kits versus self-sourcing, vendors, lead times, costs, revisions, and the SO-ARM100 and reBot-DevArm product lines.

## Fabrication and Building

**TaxonomyID:** BUILD

3D printing, fasteners, and tools, then assembling, calibrating, troubleshooting, and comparing the SO-ARM100 and reBot-DevArm.

## Python Motion Programming

**TaxonomyID:** PYMO

Hardware abstraction classes, fake arms, joint-space moves, trajectories, control loops, pick and place, teleoperation, logging, plotting, and testing.

## Kinematics and Simulation

**TaxonomyID:** KINE

Coordinate frames, forward and inverse kinematics, Cartesian motion, workspace analysis, simulation, and the ROS 2 tour.

## Perception and Learning

**TaxonomyID:** PERC

Cameras, OpenCV, calibration, mapping pixels to the arm, and learning from demonstration with LeRobot.

## AI Agents and OpenClaw

**TaxonomyID:** AGNT

The agent loop, tool calling, OpenClaw and its skills, natural-language control, planning, vision-language models, and agent interfaces such as MCP.

## Agent Safety and Evaluation

**TaxonomyID:** AGSF

Safety layers, validation, dry-run modes, prompt injection and other agent risks, and testing and evaluating agent-controlled systems.

## Projects, Operations, and Teaching

**TaxonomyID:** PROJ

Capstone projects, startup and shutdown, maintenance, classroom and makerspace use, and next steps.

## Advanced Path Mathematics

**TaxonomyID:** ADV

Optional advanced chapter: orientation math, the Jacobian, trajectory polynomials, path planning, and predicting and measuring arm paths.
