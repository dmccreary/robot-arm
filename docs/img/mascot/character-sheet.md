# Character Sheet: Servo the Robot Arm

The canonical identity document for Servo, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Controlling a Robot Arm** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of
AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to the
description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Servo
- **Species:** Robot Arm
- **Subject:** Python programming and low-cost robot arms
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's move it!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Warm orange — hex `#F57C00`
- **Accent color:** Indigo — hex `#3F51B5` (matches the book's indigo theme)
- **Face screen:** Soft cream — hex `#FFF8E1`, with two big round dark-navy eyes (white highlights) and a gentle smile
- **Clothing / accessories:** None. Small indigo bolts on the joint caps; a round indigo base disc instead of legs.
- **Anatomy:** A round orange head with a cream face screen, a short rounded orange torso on a round indigo base disc, and **one** long articulated arm (two thick orange segments joined by round indigo shoulder, elbow, and wrist caps) ending in a two-finger parallel gripper that serves as Servo's hand. No legs, no second arm.
- **Expression:** Friendly, curious, and patient
- **Size proportion:** Compact and chibi-proportioned, sized to read clearly at about 90 px
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector with clean, consistent dark-indigo outlines and soft, simple shading

## Personality

- Curious — always wants to see what happens when the code runs
- Patient — happy to repeat a step until it clicks
- Safety-minded — gently careful around power switches and moving parts
- Playful — celebrates small wins and loves a servo pun

## Voice

- Speaks in short, encouraging sentences aimed at readers aged 12 and up
- Uses movement and robot-arm metaphors (joints, angles, "one degree at a time")
- Calls readers "builders" and treats mistakes as normal calibration
- Signature phrases: "Let's move it!", "One joint at a time.", "Simulate first, then power on."

## Why This Mascot

A robot arm is the book's subject, so the mascot teaches by example: its single
gripper hand waves, points, and gives a thumbs-up the same way the readers'
SO-ARM101 will. The name *Servo* honors the servo motors that drive every joint,
is short and easy to remember for a 12-year-old, and is gender-neutral. Orange
and indigo match the site's accent and primary colors.
